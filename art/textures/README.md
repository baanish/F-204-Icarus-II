# Icarus skin maps

The compositor makes ten 4096-square PNGs (300 px/m) from generated unmarked surface art, the airframe's own panel layout, current geometry bakes and scripted scheme data. Stock skins are 2048 square at 108 to 132 px/m; the Icarus atlas keeps the same UV layout at twice the resolution. The Navy atlas is both `_Basecolor` and livery 1. Bare Metal has a separate metallic/smoothness map. Exact generated prompts sit beside every source PNG in `generated/`. The Barlow Condensed font is for review captions; `fonts/OFL.txt` carries its licence. Aircraft lettering comes from generated sheets.

Run from the repository root, stopping at any failed command:

When `generated/full/source-policy.json` is present, rebuild the source mapping with the whole-aircraft steps below before the `compose` command.

```powershell
pwsh -NoProfile -File tools/Run-Blender.ps1 --background --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python art/blender/bake_skin.py -- prepare
pwsh -NoProfile -File tools/Run-Blender.ps1 --background --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python art/blender/bake_skin.py -- audit
pwsh -NoProfile -File tools/Run-Blender.ps1 --background --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python art/blender/pilot2_skin.py -- structure
python art/textures/compose_skin.py compose
python art/textures/panel_checks.py
python art/textures/decal_checks.py
pwsh -NoProfile -File tools/Run-Blender.ps1 --background --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python tools/export_blockout.py
python tools/export_manifest.py --write
python art/textures/compose_skin.py measure
pwsh -NoProfile -File tools/Run-Blender.ps1 --background --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python art/blender/bake_skin.py -- review
python art/textures/review_skin.py
pwsh -NoProfile -File tools/Link-WorktreeAssets.ps1
pwsh -NoProfile -File tools/Build-Icarus.ps1
```

`prepare` runs the current generator and UV checks, saves `geometry.blend`, bakes signed position, shading normal and part ID twice, then AO. `atlas_geometry.npz` is the 4096 production bake (`uvs.ATLAS_SIZE`); `geometry_maps.npz` and the AO stay at 2048 (`uvs.PAINT_SIZE`) for the generated paint registration below, and compose upsamples them. Mirror pairs bake from the left only. AO evaluates 128 occlusion probes per texel in each of `skin_review.AO_SAMPLES` samples, on a fully scrambled Sobol pattern: Cycles' default blue noise correlates the probes in blocks of about 8 texels, which upsampled into blurry rectangles in the paint. Bake copies are separated by 30 metres for self AO. Each paired forward, mid and aft fuselage shell shares assembly occluders with its fuselage section, and the left wing skin shares its box. Moving parts and stations have self AO only. Trim faces bake to a separate sink image, while still occluding their own skin. If only AO needs rebuilding, `bake_skin.py -- ao` resumes from `geometry.blend`.

Bake preparation, export, damage diagrams and build-based reviews share checked aircraft scenes in `.cache/build-cache/`. Python sources, reference inputs, build parameters, Blender version, Cycles devices and build environment select the entry. A hit restores the cockpit maps too. Failed builds never publish an entry. Set `$env:ICARUS_NO_BUILD_CACHE = '1'` to rebuild through every check; remove that variable to resume reuse. Interactive `build_icarus.build()` always regenerates, while these scripts use `build_cached()` in a fresh scene.

Four 1152-square regions, overlapping by 256 pixels, assemble the accepted generated neutral base. The compositor rejects native inputs below 1152 pixels. An 18-pixel masked local mean removes broad source colour. Scripted reflected world-position fields provide the scheme. Generated detail fades over 14 atlas texels at island boundaries, and 16 texels of colour extend into gutters. Decals touch only unique forward-fuselage and fin islands.

Nozzle skin islands added after the source-atlas pilot use the accepted unmarked `bare_metal.png` through reflected world triplanar projection. That supplies fine detail where the old source silhouettes contain gutters. Scheme colour, normal relief, cavity and smoothness still come through the same compositor. `composition.json` records those parts and source coverage for every part.

`panels.py` draws relief only where the airframe has structure, from `structure.json` (`pilot2_skin.py structure`: frames, spars, ribs and the fuselage body sections). Every seam is a plane section of the skin, so it is straight on the airframe, and ends on another seam or a physical edge (a part boundary or crease). Fuselage butt joints sit on every second frame with a rivet row either side and run the whole way round the skin, over the intake fairings and the wing-root fillet down to the wing and round the belly; the frames between carry one rivet row; section breaks get a row just inside each edge; two lap joints run on the corner longerons, 45 degrees off vertical, each bay between butt joints on the plane through the 45 degree lines at its two joints, so they meet on the joints. No line is drawn where the skin lies in a seam's own plane (the speed brake wells' walls). Wing panels join on both spars with screw rows, straight in plan from the visible root to a chordwise crank rib joint on the leading-edge flap's inboard end and from there to the tip, and carry rib rivet rows between the spars, plus the upper fuel filler. The fin and stabilator have a front-spar joint and a rear-spar row; control surfaces have a hinge-side row and rib rows; their chord fractions use straight leading and trailing edges fitted to their texels. `ACCESS_PANELS` get a rounded outline and a screw row. Every feature is a distance in world metres at texel centres, evaluated a few texels past each island along its own UV derivatives (`rasterize.extend_geometry`), so lines keep one width, stay continuous over UV cuts and keep correct normals at island edges. Panel faces between features are exactly flat. The same fields give the tangent normal, cavity AO, reduced smoothness, albedo grime in joints and a faint ring at each fastener. The closed gun-port access panel has no opening. The skin aft of the butt joint at `BARE_AFT_JOINT_S` and below both upper lap joints, the speed brakes and the nozzle are unpainted metal on every livery (`panels.bare_aft`); the material changes across the bounding grooves' width.

`panel_checks.py` holds the layout to that after every compose. Closure joins texels off the shipped grooves into panels across UV cuts, and across the fuselage skin parts' boundaries where the surface continues in 3D; other part boundaries and creases are physical edges. A seam texel whose two sides are the same panel belongs to a seam that stops short; any such run over 20 mm fails. Straightness takes each straight seam piece's centreline texels (within 0.5 mm of the seam) and fails when the thinnest slab holding them is more than 2 mm thick, 1 mm either side of its plane (rounded access panel and filler outlines excepted). A least-squares plane would not do: where a seam runs along the texel grid its centreline texels come in clusters along a near-line, and that plane tilts. The numbers go to `panel_checks.json` beside the bake.

`decal_checks.py` holds every placement in `insignia.PLACEMENTS`, on both sides, to clear skin after the panel check. Each decal prints whole (every inked source pixel lands on a side-facing texel of its parts, so no leading edge or part edge clips it), stays 30 mm from its sheet's physical edges (the canopy cut and sill, control-surface gaps, the fin root), lies under no other part (canopy frame and sill rail), stays 19.2 mm from every seam centreline (off the joint and its rivet rows), 30 mm from every formation lens and rim (`structure.json` fittings), and off the Bare Metal bands. Real aircraft paint markings over butt and lap joints, so `PAINT_OVER` may name, per placement, joints it crosses on purpose; it is empty, and an access panel, canopy frame or control-surface gap is never allowed. The numbers go to `decal_checks.json`.

The generated paint keeps its broad tone only: `liveries.paint_grain` closes away the generator's own dark seams and rivets, opens away bright specks, blurs and keeps 0.45 of the residual, and the triplanar scheme detail is blurred by 8 source pixels at the lowered `wear_strength` in each livery file. Decals are flat-printed (`insignia.flat_print`: wear specks filled, colours snapped to a 1 to 3 colour palette, antialiased edges) and box-filtered with 16 world-space samples over each texel's footprint. `damage.py` projects generated soot and torn-metal source art in reflected world coordinates, then derives dent normals from it.

`measure` rejects a livery seam median above 2.26 RGB levels, checks PNG dimensions and RGB livery encoding, records current mirror checks, and writes `gate.json` with input and output hashes. A gate remains `pipeline_accepted: false` until the owner accepts it. Regenerate the gate after integrated geometry, source or export-manifest changes.

Review outputs go under `.cache/review/wave2/p9b/02/`. The review includes four views of every livery with stations loaded and empty, nose and wing-root close-ups, matched raking-light clay and Navy views, normal-and-AO clay views, open/deployed/detached AO states and full damage. Stock store meshes are local review references loaded through `tools/local-paths.json`; no stock texture is read or supplied to image generation.

After a change to one scheme, `bake_skin.py -- review --livery 3` rerenders only that livery's ten views. Rerun `review_skin.py` afterwards to refresh sheets and hashes. A complete `review` remains required when shared maps or geometry change.

The stock livery contract does not swap metallic textures. The Icarus plugin adds a reversible `UnitPart.SetLivery` postfix for livery 4. It selects `Icarus_livery_4_m.png` for Bare Metal and restores `Icarus_skin_m.png` for every other livery. An inactive mesh-free prefab child holds both material references as bundle dependencies. The postfix changes the material instance already owned by the stock damage renderer.

Unity imports SHA-checked maps and writes four Primeva `LiveryData` assets. A13 checks slot 0, damage registration, UV bounds, map dimensions and types, metallic alpha, distinct DXT1 liveries and faction ownership. Offline PNG checks do not establish Unity compression, runtime blending, detach dissolve, LOD or multiplayer behaviour. No game runs belong to this offline pipeline.

## Per-part source pilot

`pilot2_parts.py` repacks selected UV charts into dense part guides with world-axis arrows, metre bounds and structural lines. The radome, forward left side, upper wing root, upper wing and fin sources live in `generated/pilot2/`. Every call keeps its exact prompt, context pack and unchanged native output. `round2_style.txt` is the shared material policy. The existing 2048 atlas, seven-texel detail fade, eight-texel gutter padding, scheme colours and decals still apply.

After `prepare` and `audit` above, run:

```powershell
pwsh -NoProfile -File tools/Run-Blender.ps1 --background --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python art/blender/pilot2_skin.py -- structure
python art/textures/pilot2_parts.py guides
python art/textures/pilot2_parts.py compose --version round1
python art/textures/pilot2_parts.py compose --version pilot2
pwsh -NoProfile -File tools/Run-Blender.ps1 --background --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python art/blender/pilot2_skin.py -- render
python art/textures/pilot2_review.py
```

Both versions write to `.cache/review/wave2/p9b-round2/`, including separate map folders. They do not replace shipped maps. `context --name <part>` writes the context and exact prompt before a new built-in image generation call. Generate in the order above so each call can receive earlier neighbour edge strips. Source silhouettes register independently to their original UV charts and fail below 0.70 normalized shape IoU or 180 native source px/m. `stitch.json` records the actual bounds and density. Source masks and chart IDs are specific to the prepared geometry; regenerate and inspect guides if geometry changes. Fin base detail reflects to the other face in world Y/Z, while decals retain their separate readable placement. Remaining surfaces deliberately retain the existing quadrant art until the owner reviews the pilot.

The pilot applies a maintained-skin relief policy to those surfaces only: 0.3 of legacy height and cavity displacement. A legacy 0.34-texel seam becomes 0.102 texel, about 0.68 mm at 150 px/m. This is an art decision, not a measured joint depth. Paint scratches stay in albedo without reading as deep corrosion. `pilot2_review.py` pins the exact height and cavity conversion and checks that the rest of the atlas is unchanged by this policy. The comparison therefore includes the new generated base and its revised relief treatment.

## Whole aircraft per-part sources

`generated/full/source-policy.json` selects the full method for the normal `compose` command. It requires a fresh inventory and never silently substitutes quadrant art. Rebuild from the current checkout's geometry with:

When `generated/full/source-atlas/current/parts.json` exists, `pilot2_parts.py guides` restores and verifies that mapping, then writes current ownership guides to the review cache. `full_skin_guides.py inventory` also restores the persisted pack. Accepted generation guides, templates, prompts and source images remain unchanged. A changed geometry bake requires `full_skin_guides.py adapt` with its verified hash; see [the source-atlas instructions](generated/full/source-atlas/README.md).

```powershell
pwsh -NoProfile -File tools/Rebuild-Skin.ps1 -Render -BuildPlugin
```

The script runs prepare, audit, structural extraction, guides, inventory, coverage checks, native registration and production composition in order, stopping at the first failure. It writes ten PNGs to `art/export/textures`, stages identical review copies, refreshes the export manifest and `gate.json`, then checks coverage, normals, AO, seams and Bare Metal masks. Damage uses the existing generated soot and torn-metal art projected onto the current geometry, so a repack cannot retain stale damage UVs. `-Render` adds 16 current-geometry livery overviews, then `art/blender/skin_closeups.py` renders 2560 x 1440 matched overviews and close-ups of all four liveries (nose markings, modex, roundel, fuselage panel field, wing panel, aft fuselage) plus clay views to `.cache/review/share/texture_art`. `-BuildPlugin` builds the Release DLL under `.cache/build-staging/dotnet`. Commands and exit codes go to `full/rebuild-commands.json`. It launches neither Unity nor the game. Run B0 after this chain and ship the rebuilt bundle with the plugin DLL.

After a geometry or UV change, `inventory` and `restore` rebuild the current mapping automatically from the frozen bake in `generated/full/source-atlas/native/generation-inputs`. They reuse the existing maps when both bake and atlas hashes match. No snapshot path, hash editing or guide regeneration is needed. Native registration and companion validation still run on the immutable generation atlas. One world-space index map carries base, height, roughness, both validity fields and used coverage into the current atlas. Current group masks describe ownership, while the original chart masks and generation guides describe native registration. `guides` writes current ownership guides to the review cache and preserves the original inputs.

The `be45099` mapping covers 60 canonical parts and 2,342,611 texels. Position distance at most 15 mm and normal cosine at least 0.95 establish reuse for 2,274,405 texels. The remaining 68,206 receive recorded nearest-parent paint. Replaced gear doors and speed-brake panels always use the parent-paint policy. Brake wells and actuators use the mechanical trim material outside the skin atlas. All current skin texels are assigned; no new mechanical-detail claim is made for transferred paint.

An unknown new skin part receives one nearest physical parent, selected by RMS nearest world-position and normal distance across its texels with a lexical name tie break. The donor and scores are recorded. All its texels carry the parent-paint limitation; none claims matched mechanical detail. Named door donor rules remain explicit. Native source IoU, density and companion thresholds remain unchanged. `adapt --source-snapshot PATH --bake-sha256 HASH` remains a bootstrap command for importing a different original source atlas.

For overview-only review after a geometry repack, render `full_skin_review.py -- --overview-only`, then run `full_skin_checks.py sheets --overview-only`. This requires all 16 current livery/view combinations and excludes historical close-ups. `bare_metal_checks.py --current-maps` checks exact painted, aluminium and protected material values without comparing texels from different atlases.

The generation atlas has 33 source groups covering 56 original canonical parts through direct chart masks and ordered geometric transfers. Five pilot sources are reused. New groups live in `generated/full/` beside their exact prompts, contexts, labelled guides and generation records. Saved generation inputs are immutable. Physical neighbours supply paint-edge samples, while frames, spars and the access-panel list supply the structural guide. Fin mirror regions outside the measured tolerance receive nearest parent paint and carry no matched-detail claim. The current mapping has its own part inventory and coverage counts.

Four bases have explicit source stitching records. The canopy and forward lower fuselage retain their generated paint fitted to exact chart templates. Two rejected generation requests, lower front wing and pylon plug, use bounded grain from our generated style anchor, with measured guide structure where available. They are material reuse, not independently generated part detail. Raw generation outputs remain unchanged. `full_skin_source_stitch.py` reproduces these base-authoring operations before any companion is generated.

Each accepted final base has native height and roughness requests. `full_skin_calls.py companion NAME --base PATH --kind height` writes the exact call pack; use `roughness` for the other channel. `record` preserves the unmodified native output, and `failure` records an explicit service failure. Generation records are kept with the sources and restored to the current inventory folder on `inventory`.

`companion_maps.py` checks the native image without shifting, resizing or warping it. Acceptance requires a cross-correlation offset below one texel with a 0.1-texel guard band, plus 0.75 positional precision and recall for both seam centrelines and one-to-one fastener centres within one pixel. Pixel-area scores are secondary diagnostics. Sparse parts can fail for insufficient evidence. Every failure keeps its measured report and independently falls back to the base-luminance extraction.

Companion height and roughness no longer feed production maps: relief and smoothness come from the airframe layout in `panels.py`. The companion validation still runs and its evidence is kept. `alignment.json`, `stitch.json`, `composition.json`, measurements and render manifests record the actual decisions. These offline checks do not establish Unity compression or runtime behaviour.

Livery 4 adds sheet tones and subtle brushing through `bare_metal.py`, one tone and polish per structural skin panel from the `panels.py` layout, so sheet boundaries sit on the drawn joints. Its dedicated map has red 0.98 on bare aluminium, with the exact accumulated decal alpha, band coverage and anti-glare mask making paint dielectric. The radome stays dielectric, and the unpainted aft fuselage and nozzle blend in by the shared map's seam-bounded bare weight. Glossiness is 0 so the dedicated alpha controls polished sheets and rougher anti-glare paint. Installed D3D11 AircraftSkin bytecode uses `saturate(max(metallicAlpha, glossiness))` for undamaged opaque liveries; gloss 1 would override the rough paint.

`art/blender/bare_metal_review.py`, run through the Blender wrapper, renders matched bright-daylight runway views from shipped round-1 maps, the saved prior full set, the dark shared-map revision and the dedicated-map revision. `bare_metal_checks.py --maps-only` checks native material masks before rendering. Without that flag it also verifies renders and writes side-by-side PNGs and the Bare Metal handback section. Comparisons require the retained `full/bare-metal/before` and `dielectric-before` snapshots. `full_skin_checks.py sheets` verifies the maps each view consumes and records changes to unused maps without rewriting original render hashes. After a production compose, regenerate the export manifest and measurement gate including all ten maps before B0. Ship the rebuilt bundle with the plugin DLL that provides the swap.
